# Institution logos

These files identify the corresponding institutions in the interface.

- `american-express.svg`: unchanged [American Express blue-box logo](https://www.aexp-static.com/cdaas/one/statics/axp-static-assets/1.8.0/package/dist/img/logos/dls-logo-bluebox-solid.svg), linked by the [American Express homepage](https://www.americanexpress.com/).
- `onepay.svg`: unchanged [OnePay app tile](https://images.ctfassets.net/e3a511gf6wvh/tKohLIq3t6T2ktjnk5hIi/ae4e85f2fbb71437d54a84be1c955d20/Tile.svg), linked by the [OnePay homepage](https://www.onepay.com/).
- `capital-one.svg`: inline logo from the [Capital One homepage](https://www.capitalone.com/), with framework attributes removed; artwork and colors are unchanged.
- `pnc.svg`: circular emblem extracted from the [official PNC SVG logo](https://d1io3yog0oux5.cloudfront.net/_089afc4ce03f3c8175c939c4de65c807/pnc/db/2366/22944/image.svg), linked by the [PNC investor site](https://investor.pnc.com/). The wordmark is omitted and the view box fits the emblem; the emblem's paths and colors are unchanged.
- `paypal.svg`: unchanged [PayPal color mark](https://www.paypalobjects.com/paypal-ui/logos/svg/paypal-mark-color.svg) from PayPal's official asset CDN.

Downloaded November 16, 2026. The logos remain the property of their respective owners.
